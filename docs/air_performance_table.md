## Key Performance Insights

### 🏆 Top Performers (>50,000 m³ average)
1. **Canada** - Arctic advantage with 347 study locations
2. **Tajikistan** - Exceptional high-altitude performance (3,245m)
3. **Pakistan** - Highest maximum volume recorded (235,355 m³)

### 🎯 Technology Origin Validation
- **India (Ladakh)**: 31,846 m³ average, proving technology viability
- **32°N latitude, 2,366m altitude**: Optimal mid-latitude/high-altitude combination
- **338 days survival**: Excellent operational duration

### 📊 Geographic Patterns

#### **Latitude Effect**
- **>60°N**: Consistently high performance (Canada, Norway, Sweden, Iceland)
- **45-60°N**: Good performance with moderate altitude requirements
- **30-45°N**: Requires high altitude (>2000m) for success
- **±10° Equator**: Complete failure regardless of altitude

#### **Altitude Compensation**
- **High latitudes (>60°N)**: Successful at any altitude
- **Mid-latitudes (30-45°N)**: Need >2000m elevation
- **Tropical regions**: Failed even at high altitudes (2000m+)

### ❌ Failed Regions Analysis
- **Tropical highlands**: Ecuador (1,720m), Ethiopia (1,577m), Colombia (1,362m)
- **Issue**: Insufficient temperature differential for sustained ice formation
- **Conclusion**: Latitude more critical than altitude in tropical zones

### 🌍 Global Scalability
- **Arctic/Sub-Arctic** (>60°N): 6 countries, 100% success rate
- **Temperate Mountains** (30-60°N): 10 countries, 90% success rate  
- **Subtropical Mountains** (20-40°N): 4 countries, 100% success rate
- **Tropical** (±20°): 5 countries, 0% success rate

## Methodology Notes
- **Data Source**: ERA5 reanalysis (2001-2020)
- **Model**: Bulk energy and mass balance framework
- **Validation**: Drone surveys in Switzerland and India
- **Locations**: Mountain regions >500m elevation
- **Performance Metrics**: Ice volume, survival days, water efficiency

## Applications for Site Selection
1. **Optimal Zone**: Latitudes >45°N at any reasonable altitude
2. **Moderate Zone**: 30-45°N requiring >2000m elevation
3. **Avoid**: Tropical regions regardless of elevation
4. **Special Consideration**: India/Pakistan/Afghanistan demonstrate excellent subtropical mountain potential
